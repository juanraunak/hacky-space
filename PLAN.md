# Hacky Space — the plan (v4): one lesson

**The whole game teaches ONE thing, in baby words:**

> Heavy things bend space, like a ball on a blanket. That bend is gravity.
> And down in the bend, **time runs slower**.

## Part 1 — the cabin (DONE, do not touch)
Everything until you sit in the pilot seat and take off stays exactly as it
is: walking, Einstein and Newton's bubbles, the blanket, the collapse, Newton
pulled into the black hole, "We have to find Newton.", multiplayer.

## Part 2 — fly through space
- You take off. Newton got **flung past the black hole onto a blue planet** on
  the far side. The black hole sits **in the way** and pulls on you.
- On the way: a ringed gas giant, a pulsar, twin stars, a comet, and a
  **wormhole shortcut** that comes out next to Newton's planet.
- Einstein talks (bubble beside your ship) **when you look at** each thing.
- No boss cards, no pop-ups.

## Part 3 — the lesson: time
- **Newton's photo** sits in the corner: "NEWTON · age 44".
- The closer you skim the black hole, the slower your time runs, so the faster
  Newton ages: his wig greys, his beard grows, the photo shakes. The screen
  edges glow blue.
- Real physics: clock rate sqrt(1 - rs/r), exaggerated so it shows in minutes.
- Fall in and **you die**: nothing comes out, not even light. Try again; Newton's
  time kept going.

## Part 4 — you reach Newton (the end)
- Newton (as old as you made him): "What took you so long? I have waited here
  N years!"
- Einstein: "For us it was only M minutes. Near the black hole, our time ran
  slow."
- Einstein: "Heavy things bend space. That bend is gravity. And in the bend,
  time runs slower."
- The end: "You: M minutes. Newton: N years older." Play again.
